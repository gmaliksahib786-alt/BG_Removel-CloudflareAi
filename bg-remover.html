Frequently asked questions

Is this tool really free?

Yes, completely free. No account, no credit card, no watermark. Powered by Cloudflare Workers AI which gives generous free usage.

What image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP — up to 10MB per image. For best results use high-resolution images with clear subjects.

Are my images stored or shared?

No. Images are processed in real-time on Cloudflare's edge network and never stored on any server. Your privacy is fully protected.

What does the output look like?

You get a transparent PNG with only the subject remaining. The background becomes fully transparent — compatible with any design tool.

Which AI model powers this?

We use Cloudflare Workers AI's image segmentation model (BiRefNet/RMBG) — the same technology used by Cloudflare Images background removal feature.
