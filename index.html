Frequently asked questions

Is this really free and unlimited?

Yes, 100% free and unlimited. The AI model runs in your browser — there's no server to pay for. No account, no credit card, no limits ever.

Why does first use take time?

First time, RMBG-1.4 model (~45MB quantized) downloads to your browser cache. After that it's instant — even works offline.

Are my images stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Images never leave your device — no uploads, no cloud, no tracking.

What formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP — up to 20MB. For best results use clear, well-lit images with distinct subjects.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes! Mobile is slower since WASM runs on CPU. Desktop gives best speed (2-5 seconds per image).
